rtm 26.1.1 is a patch release for the 26.1 line. It makes rtm follow the todo.txt format more
closely and keep each line as it was written: lines it cannot interpret, the line's layout and
spacing, and the file's final newline are kept; only YYYY-MM-DD words are read as dates, `pri:` is
the priority only on completed lines, and a line that starts with a space is never read as complete
or prioritized. Recurrence now uses calendar months and years, skips weekends for business days,
works without a due date, keeps a count of 1 (`rec:1w`) and moves the threshold date (`t:`) with
the item; items whose threshold date has passed are shown, date filters read the threshold date,
and an item whose threshold date is after its due date is refused. On Windows, the app started from
the installer's last screen syncs, and file paths show without the `\\?\` prefix.
